1. Who We Are

SMU is an AI-powered content creation, scheduling and publishing workspace for creators and businesses operated by podgeaisolutions.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information needed to operate SMU, including:

  • account details such as email address and password hash;
  • registration, billing and account-access details you choose to provide;
  • content you create in SMU, including captions, prompts, posts and calendar data;
  • Brand Briefs, Content Packs and feedback you choose to provide;
  • connected-account settings required to support publishing workflows;
  • technical information such as logs needed to maintain reliability and security.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • operate user accounts and subscription access;
  • create, improve, schedule and publish content at your request;
  • provide Brand Brief, Content Pack, AI Studio and calendar features;
  • review feedback and improve the product;
  • monitor service reliability, diagnose errors and protect SMU from misuse;
  • communicate with users and applicants about SMU.

4. AI And Content

SMU uses AI tools to assist with content generation, image generation, caption improvement and content repurposing. When you use these features, relevant prompts, captions, Brand Brief information or source content may be sent to the AI provider so the requested feature can run.

You remain responsible for reviewing and approving content before publishing.

5. Connected Accounts

SMU may store platform connection status, webhook settings and OAuth details needed to support publishing. We do not intentionally expose passwords, webhook URLs, API keys or access tokens in public pages or diagnostic output.

6. Sharing Information

SMU shares information only where needed to provide the product, such as with hosting, database, AI, media storage, automation and social platform providers. We do not sell personal information.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect SMU data. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid storing content or secrets they do not want processed by the platform.

8. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to operate SMU, provide the service, resolve issues, meet legal obligations and improve SMU. Application, account and feedback data may be retained to manage access, support and product development.

9. Your Choices

You may choose what content to create, what platform connections to configure and whether to submit feedback or application details. You can contact SMU to ask about access, correction or deletion of information associated with the product.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact SMU through the Contact page.
