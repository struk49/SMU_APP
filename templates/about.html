About SMU

SMU is an AI-powered social-media content workspace for creators and businesses.

Create, repurpose, improve, schedule and publish social content from one place. SMU brings ideas, TikTok videos, existing content, captions, media creation and scheduling into one practical workflow.

Product focus

  • One workspace for creation, repurposing, scheduling and publishing.
  • Brand tools that help keep social content aligned.
  • Practical AI assistance with human review before publishing.

Why SMU Exists

Content creation often means switching between separate tools for writing, images, repurposing, scheduling, publishing and brand consistency. SMU's goal is to bring these workflows together so regular content work feels simpler and easier to manage.

Who SMU Is For

SMU is designed for people who create and publish content regularly without wanting to manage lots of disconnected tools.

Platform Approach

SMU connects to supported social platforms so users can publish content they choose. Current publishing support includes Instagram workflows, Facebook workflows and LinkedIn personal-profile text and single-image workflows.

Platform capabilities depend on third-party APIs and permissions.

AI That Assists, Not Replaces

AI helps generate ideas, captions and images, but users remain in control. SMU is built around review before publishing, and Brand tools help keep content aligned. AI output should be treated as assistance rather than guaranteed fact.

Privacy And Trust

Users control what they create and publish. Connected accounts can be disconnected, and SMU does not require social-network passwords.

Data handling is explained in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Built Around Real Content Workflows

SMU is being built around the way creators and businesses actually create content: starting with an idea or existing piece of content, improving it, turning it into platform-ready posts, and managing the publishing workflow from one place.

The product will keep improving based on how people use it and the feedback they provide.

Future Direction

Where SMU Is Going

The long-term direction is an AI Content Operating System. Future direction may include AI Content Planning, AI Research, Analytics, AI Memory, broader platform publishing and workflow automation.

One idea → AI assists creation → content is improved → content is scheduled → content is published → performance is analysed later → future content improves later
